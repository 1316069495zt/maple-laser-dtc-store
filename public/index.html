Material Integrity

Why We Never Compromise on Solid North American Timber.

Unlike mass-produced bamboo boards glued with formaldehyde resins, every Atelier Nord piece begins as rough-sawn, FSC-certified Canadian Hard Maple or American Black Walnut cured to 7% equilibrium moisture.

Our dual-stage optical laser pyrography vaporizes wood fibers to a precise 0.40mm relief depth before three coats of warm organic beeswax and fractionated coconut oil lock in the grain for generations.
